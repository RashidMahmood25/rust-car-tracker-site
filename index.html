How it works

Three steps, that's all

1

Place the phone in the car

Any smartphone, connected to a charger, somewhere it can see the sky — the dashboard is perfect for a strong GPS signal.

2

Press Start Live Tracking

Allow location permission once. The car marker appears on the map and follows every turn, with live speed and distance.

3

Follow the journey

Watch the route draw itself on the map. Press Stop when you arrive, or Clear Route to start a fresh trip.

Good to know: keep this browser tab open while tracking — like every website, tracking pauses if the tab is closed. A full always-on app version is on the roadmap.
