Privacy Policy — Rust Tracker

By Rust Wheel · Last updated: 1 November 2026

Rust Tracker is a web app that turns a smartphone into a live GPS car tracker. This page explains, in plain words, what information the app uses and what it does not.

Sign-in information

To see the live location, you sign in with Google, with your email and password, or with your phone number (SMS code). Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We receive your name, email address, phone number and profile photo from the method you choose, only to show your account inside the app. We never see your Google password, and your email password is stored securely by Firebase — never in plain text.

Location data

Live tracking uses your phone's own GPS, inside your browser. In the current version, your location is processed on your device to draw the map, route, speed and distance on your own screen. We do not run a server that collects or stores your location history.

Cookies and local storage

The app saves only small preferences on your device (such as your dark/light theme choice and your sign-in session) so the app works properly. No advertising trackers are used.

What we never do

We never sell your personal information, and we never share it with third parties for advertising.

Data deletion

You can delete your data at any time:

  • Sign out from the app to end your session on that device.
  • Revoke access in your Google Account (Security → Third-party access) if you signed in with Google.
  • Delete your sign-in record completely: message us on our Facebook page or LinkedIn with the email address you signed up with and the words "Delete my Rust Tracker account" — we will delete your authentication record.
